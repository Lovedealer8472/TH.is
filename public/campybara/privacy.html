Campybara Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 July 2026

Campybara ("the app") is published by Tölvuhvíslarinn ehf., Iceland. Contact: campybara@tolvuhvislarinn.is

Summary

Campybara works without an account. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or advertising identifiers, and we show no ads.

Location data

The app may request foreground location permission so it can centre the map on you and show nearby parking, campsites, and points of interest. Location is processed on your device and sent only as latitude/longitude query parameters to our own API to fetch nearby results. We do not store a history of your location on our servers.

If you plan a trip, the start and destination coordinates are sent to our routing API to calculate a road route. Our server forwards those coordinates — and nothing else — to a routing provider to compute the path. No account, device identifier, or location history is attached.

Offline pack & Google Play Billing

The app is free to use online. The optional Offline pack (product id campybara_pro) is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase token and product identifier from Google to unlock the feature on your device; we never receive your card number. Purchase state is verified on the device through the Google Play Billing Library.

Offline downloads

Downloaded campsite and parking data is stored locally in the app's database on your phone. It is not uploaded to us.

Support email

If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to send (message body, your address) and use it only to respond.

Third parties

  • Google Maps & Places — map tiles and place search when online, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Google Play — in-app purchases.
  • Routing provider — receives start/destination coordinates only, forwarded by our server when you plan a trip.
  • Our API — serves public parking/camping/POI data; queries include coordinates, never identity.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
